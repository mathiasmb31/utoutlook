Version 4.0.41 — 17 novembre 2026

Sign-in and 2FA work reliably again on the phone: no more login loop after 
quitting/relaunching, no more "header too long" error after the Authenticator 
approval, no more empty message list after rotating the screen, and closing the 
app no longer leaves hidden processes running. A very difficult debugging 
session went into fixing these — thanks for testing each build on the phone.

Tested on Fairphone 5 (FP5) with Ubuntu Touch framework 24.04 — no guarantee on other models.
Thanks for your feedback! :)
