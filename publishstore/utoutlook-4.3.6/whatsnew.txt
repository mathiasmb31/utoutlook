Version 4.3.6 — 20 November 2026

A very special thank you to the person who first reported the transparent
window bug on the OpenStore. Your clear report was the starting point of a
long and difficult investigation, and this release is the direct result of
it. Thank you for taking the time to test and to speak up.

Finding the real cause was unusually hard. The app looked frozen, but the
process was still running - the phone shell had quietly lost track of its
window. Several fixes were needed, one after the other: the app icon is back
to normal, the window no longer disappears when you rotate the phone, the
start-up screen no longer gets stuck, sign-in cookies no longer flood at
load time, the list buttons are clearer, and closing and relaunching the app
is reliable again. Unplugging the USB cable no longer makes the app appear
to crash either.

Tested on Fairphone 5 (FP5) with Ubuntu Touch framework 24.04. This is the
version I use every day, so it is the one I can confirm works. On other
phones, and on other Ubuntu Touch releases or framework updates, results
may differ - please keep that in mind when you try it.

Thanks for your feedback! :)

Changes since 4.0.93:

- Fix: the app icon (a yellow "?" could appear next to the real one).
- Fix: the app window could become invisible while the app was still
  running - it now comes back reliably.
- Fix: rotating the phone no longer freezes the app or its message list.
- Fix: the start-up screen could stay stuck instead of opening the mailbox.
- Fix: a flood of sign-in cookies could make the login page invisible.
- Fix: closing and relaunching the app is now reliable, with a proper
  "goodbye" message at the right time.
- Fix: unplugging or reconnecting USB no longer makes the app appear to
  crash.
- New: clearer buttons in the message list (Ubuntu Touch badge, two-finger
  hint, full labels).
- New: Catalan and Basque added - ten languages in total.
